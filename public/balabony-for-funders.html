Balabony — Ukrainian stories and tales for everyone

We are building Balabony — a Ukrainian-language platform of stories and tales that can be read and listened to. Its distinctive feature is voice-first access (Zero-UI): you can use the platform mostly by voice — listening and giving spoken commands, almost without touching the screen.

Our goal is to restore access to Ukrainian literature, literacy and cognitive development for the people the ordinary interface leaves behind: displaced families, veterans, older people and people with disabilities.

The problem

Millions of Ukrainians are cut off from culture and learning not by a lack of content, but by an interface they cannot use: small screens, text-first apps, limited vision or motor ability, low digital literacy. The war has deepened this — children of displaced and frontline families have lost stable schooling, and many isolated readers have no comfortable way to reach Ukrainian-language content at all.

The solution

The platform is being built around voice-first control (Zero-UI) — an approach where the person listens and navigates by voice, with the screen and buttons reduced to a minimum. This makes literature and learning usable hands-free and almost screen-free — for a child without a stable school, a veteran in recovery, an older reader, or a person with a vision or motor impairment.

We bring together:

  • original Ukrainian stories and tales (text and professional audio);
  • a practical Ukrainian spelling guide — in plain language, with a separate children's version (already available on the platform);
  • cognitive games — exercises for attention, memory, language and logic;
  • accessibility-first design (WCAG 2.1 AA);
  • an offline bridge: QR codes printed in our newspapers connect families without reliable internet to audio content through any camera phone.

Cognitive games

Beyond reading and listening, Balabony offers a set of cognitive games for training attention, memory, language and logical thinking, built on Ukrainian language and cultural material. They include exercises for working memory and verbal fluency, games for spatial thinking and focus, and classic logic games (chess, draughts, dominoes, sudoku, backgammon) with several difficulty levels. The games are voice-accessible and suit different ages — from children learning to read to older users who want to keep their minds active. They are part of the same Ukrainian-language environment, not a separate product.

Who it serves

Children of internally displaced people and de-occupied communities, children of veterans' and wounded servicemembers' families, children with disabilities, families in rural areas without reliable internet access, and older readers.

Technology

The AI voice and reading layer — Ukrainian-language speech synthesis, voice navigation, and adaptive reading — is in development. The platform's working foundation is already live, including the Ukrainian spelling guide and the cognitive games; technology readiness is at TRL 6.

What sets us apart

Over 20 years of work with Ukraine's least-reached communities and our own distribution network: four print titles with a circulation of around 1.56 million copies per year via the national postal service. This network is a bridge from print readers to the digital platform through QR codes.

Transparency and social mission

Balabony is a social project. We will maintain a principle of separate accounting for grant funding and platform revenue: grant and donor funds will be directed exclusively to mission purposes (free and reduced-cost access for vulnerable groups, and the creation of accessible content) and will not be mixed with commercial subscription revenue. This will ensure the targeted use of funders' money and transparent reporting.

Status

Concept-to-prototype stage. The launch timeline depends on grant funding for the project and the support of donors.
